FAQ

Answers to technical questions.

At launch, Trace observes native macOS Safari, Google Chrome, Apple Notes, Slack, and your terminal workdirs. Support for Obsidian, Notion, and Cursor workspaces is currently in beta testing.
No mandatory cloud AI is required. All indexing, vectorization, and natural query synthesis are executed on-device using optimized Apple Silicon Metal accelerators. External AI handoff is 100% user-directed.
Mac-first lets us take advantage of unified memory architecture and strict local privacy sandboxing. As we mature, opt-in team sync will allow teams to share project knowledge graphs with encrypted access control.
We onboard rolling batches every Thursday to preserve our direct support channel with developers and researchers. Priority is granted to early engineers testing on macOS Sonoma and Sequoia.
