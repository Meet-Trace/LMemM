Frequently asked.

Does Trace send my screenshots to the cloud? expand_more

No. Trace leverages Apple Silicon Neural Engines (M1-M4) for local OCR, parsing, and vector embeddings. Raw memory images never leave your local SSD.

What is the impact on battery life and RAM? expand_more

Trace sits idle until window context shifts or keystroke pauses occur. It typically consumes less than 1.2% CPU and roughly 180MB RAM on standard Apple Silicon configurations.

When will Windows or Linux be supported? expand_more

We are dedicated to building a native, high-performance macOS companion first. Windows (NPU Copilot+) and Linux clients are scheduled for late 2025.
