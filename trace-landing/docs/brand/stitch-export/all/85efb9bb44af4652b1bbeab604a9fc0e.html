Frequently Asked Questions

Does my data ever leave my Mac? expand_more

No. All embedding generation, vector indexing, and memory summarization run strictly on Apple Silicon using local CoreML and Metal acceleration. No telemetry containing your files leaves your device.

What is the battery and CPU impact? expand_more

Trace utilizes the Apple Neural Engine (ANE) on low-priority QoS threads. Idle power usage is virtually imperceptible (< 1% CPU), and indexing automatically pauses when your laptop is under 20% battery.

Which versions of macOS are supported? expand_more

Trace is engineered for macOS Sonoma (14.0+) and macOS Sequoia (15.0+), optimized for M1, M2, M3, and M4 Apple Silicon architectures.

Can I search private browsing sessions? expand_more

No. Private windows in Safari, Chrome, and Arc are automatically ignored by default to preserve absolute operational safety.
