Questions

Frequently asked

Does Trace record my screen 24/7? expand_more

No. Trace uses lightweight native hooks and structured text APIs rather than heavy, battery-draining continuous video recordings. It captures context metadata only when you transition between tasks.

Where is my data stored? expand_more

Your index is encrypted on your local macOS filesystem using Apple Secure Enclave. Sync between your own devices uses end-to-end encryption with zero-knowledge relays.

How does it impact Mac battery life? expand_more

Trace is written natively in Swift and Metal. In typical daily tests, it consumes less than 1.5% of overall system energy, running at idle priority during intensive compiles or video rendering.

When will Windows & Linux arrive? expand_more

We are focused on delivering an exceptional macOS experience first. Windows support is scheduled for late 2025.
